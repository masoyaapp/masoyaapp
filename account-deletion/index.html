Delete Your Masoya Account

You can permanently delete your Masoya account at any time. Follow the steps below, and learn what happens to your data.

1 How to Delete Your Account

To delete your account, open the Masoya app and follow these steps:

  1. Go to Profile.
  2. Tap the Settings icon.
  3. Select Privacy & Security.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion request.

Once confirmed, your account deletion will begin processing immediately.

2 What Happens When You Delete Your Account

When you delete your account:

  • Your profile will be permanently removed.
  • Your photos and bio will be deleted.
  • Your matches and chat messages will be deleted.
  • Your authentication account will be deleted.

This action is irreversible. Make sure you really want to leave before confirming.

3 Data Deletion and Retention

When you delete your Masoya account, your account and associated personal data are permanently deleted from our systems.

  • Your profile information is deleted.
  • Your photos and bio are deleted.
  • Your matches and chat messages are deleted.
  • Your authentication account is deleted.

We do not retain your personal data after account deletion, except where retention is required by applicable law.
